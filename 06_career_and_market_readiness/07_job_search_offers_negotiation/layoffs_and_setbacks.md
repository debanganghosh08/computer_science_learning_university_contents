# Layoffs and setbacks

| Goal | Time | Level | Back to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Handle rejections, long searches, withdrawn offers, performance plans and layoffs with a clear checklist, protect your money and health, and get back into the search | Read before you need it; first-week checklist ~4 hours | L2 → L4 | [Job search hub](README.md) |

> **Not legal, tax or immigration advice.** Rules differ by country, employer size, contract and visa type, and they change. Use the official sources linked and get professional advice for decisions that matter.

Setbacks are normal in a technology career. Hiring cycles turn: US software development postings peaked in February 2022 and fell by about three-quarters by mid-2025 before partly recovering ([market map](../01_market_map/README.md#3-entry-level-hiring-what-the-data-shows)). Many capable engineers have been laid off or rejected for reasons that had little to do with their work. What you control is how prepared you are and how quickly you recover.

## 1. Rejections

- **Debrief every rejection** with the template in the [pipeline page](applications_pipeline.md#5-interview-logistics). Look for patterns across five or more, not single events.
- **Ask once for feedback** (template in [follow-ups](applications_pipeline.md#3-follow-ups)). Many companies will not give it; that is policy, not a judgement on you.
- **Reapplying.** Many companies have a waiting period before you can interview again for a similar role, often several months (common practice; varies, ask the recruiter). Use it to fix the weakness the interview exposed.
- **Separate the outcome from the performance.** You can interview well and still lose to a candidate with closer experience. Judge yourself on what you prepared and how you performed.

## 2. Long searches and gaps

If the search runs past three months:

1. **Diagnose with numbers** (see [when to change strategy](applications_pipeline.md#6-when-to-change-strategy)).
2. **Keep producing evidence.** A gap filled with a shipped project, open-source contributions, a reproduced paper or freelance work is easy to explain. A gap filled with only applications is not.
3. **Widen carefully.** Add adjacent roles from your role page's "Adjacent roles and transitions" section (for example, a backend engineer adding platform or data engineering roles), or adjacent employer types (GCCs, services firms with strong teams, funded startups).
4. **Explain the gap briefly and honestly** in interviews: "I graduated in May and have been searching while building X; here is what I shipped." Then move to the work.

## 3. A delayed or withdrawn offer

Offers for new graduates are sometimes delayed or withdrawn when employers' plans change.

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Get the change **in writing** (email is fine), including any new joining date |
| 2 | Ask specific questions: Is the offer still valid? What is the expected date? Is there any compensation for the delay? Will I be informed monthly? |
| 3 | **Restart your pipeline immediately.** Do not wait for the delayed date. Accepting another job while an offer is indefinitely delayed is a reasonable decision; tell the first employer politely if you do |
| 4 | Tell your college placement cell; some colleges have rules or support for this situation |
| 5 | Check your offer letter for clauses on withdrawal and joining-date changes; if you resigned elsewhere based on the offer and lost money, get legal advice on whether you have a claim |

## 4. Performance improvement plans

A performance improvement plan (PIP) is a formal written plan with goals and a deadline. Sometimes it is a genuine effort to help; sometimes it documents a decision already made. Respond as if it is genuine, and prepare as if it is not.

1. **Get specifics in writing:** measurable goals, the time frame, who decides, and how progress is assessed.
2. **Ask for weekly check-ins** and send a short written summary after each one.
3. **Do the work** and keep your own record (links to pull requests, tickets, feedback).
4. **In parallel, update your resume and start conversations** quietly. It is better to have options than to need them.
5. **Read your contract and company policy** on notice, severance and how a PIP outcome affects them.

## 5. Layoffs: the first-week checklist

### 5.1 Everyone

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Personal copies of your offer letter, appointment letter, last 6 payslips, tax documents, performance reviews and any written praise | You may lose system access immediately |
| The termination or separation letter, with the last working day and the reason stated as a layoff or position elimination | Background checks and future employers may ask |
| What you will be paid and when: notice pay or severance, unused leave encashment, prorated bonus, reimbursements | Check against policy and contract |
| Equity: which shares or options are vested, the exercise window for options, and the deadline | Unexercised options can expire within weeks |
| Health insurance end date | Arrange personal cover before it lapses |
| References: ask your manager and a senior colleague now, while the work is fresh | They will be glad to help soon after; it is harder months later |
| Personal contacts of colleagues | People from your team are your best referral network |

Do not sign a separation agreement in a hurry if it includes a waiver of claims. Read it, ask questions, and get advice if the amounts are significant.

### 5.2 India

- **Full and final settlement.** Ask for a written statement of every component, the payment date, and your **relieving and experience letters**.
- **Provident fund.** Keep your UAN active and transfer the balance to your next employer when you join; transferring keeps your service history continuous. Withdrawal rules for unemployment changed during 2025, so check the current rules on the [EPFO site](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/) before withdrawing.
- **Gratuity.** Payable if you meet the service requirement (normally five years for permanent employees; one year for fixed-term employees under the labour codes in force from 21 November 2025).
- **Retrenchment protections.** Under the Industrial Relations Code, 2020, retrenched "workers" are entitled to retrenchment compensation, and the code adds a reskilling fund into which employers pay 15 days of wages per retrenched worker, credited within 45 days, in addition to retrenchment compensation ([DLA Piper, updated Feb 2026](https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2025/government-of-india-notifies-the-labour-codes-ushers-a-new-era-of-compliances)). Whether you count as a "worker" depends on your role and pay (employees in managerial or supervisory roles above a wage threshold may be excluded), and some rules were still being finalised in 2026. Get advice if the amounts matter.
- **Tax.** Severance and notice pay are generally taxable as salary; collect Form 16 from the employer.
- **Health.** Group cover usually ends with employment. Personal policies can have waiting periods for pre-existing conditions, so buy one before you need it.

### 5.3 United States

- **WARN Act.** Employers covered by the federal Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification Act must generally give 60 days' notice of plant closings and mass layoffs; several states have their own versions ([US Department of Labor](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta/layoffs/warn)).
- **Health coverage.** COBRA may let you continue employer health coverage for a period, usually at your own cost ([US Department of Labor](https://www.dol.gov/general/topic/health-plans/cobra)).
- **Unemployment insurance** is run by each state; apply quickly.

### 5.4 Visa holders in the US

- **H-1B and similar statuses** (E-1, E-2, E-3, H-1B1, L-1, O-1, TN): after employment ends, US rules allow a grace period of up to 60 consecutive days, or until the end of the authorised validity period if that is sooner, once per validity period ([8 CFR 214.1(l)(2)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/8/214.1)). In that time you may find a new employer to file a petition, change status, or prepare to leave. You may not work during the grace period unless otherwise authorised.
- **F-1 students on OPT:** post-completion OPT allows a limited number of days of unemployment (90 days, with additional days for STEM OPT extensions) ([8 CFR 214.2(f)(10)(ii)(E)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/8/214.2)).
- Speak to an immigration lawyer in the first week. Deadlines are strict.

### 5.5 Health

Losing a job is stressful, and stress affects sleep, judgement and relationships. Keep a routine (sleep, exercise, meals, a fixed daily start time), talk to people you trust, and get help early if you feel low for more than a couple of weeks. In India, the government's **Tele-MANAS** mental-health helpline is **14416** ([Tele-MANAS](https://telemanas.mohfw.gov.in/)); in the US, call or text **988**.

## 6. Money

- **Emergency fund.** A common guideline is three to six months of essential expenses in a savings account before you take on risky moves (a startup, freelancing, a long notice period). Build it from your first job.
- **Know your monthly burn:** rent, food, transport, loan payments, family support, insurance. That number tells you how long you can search.
- **Avoid high-interest debt** (credit cards, personal loans, buy-now-pay-later) to cover a search. Cut costs first.
- **Temporary income** from freelancing or teaching can extend your runway ([freelancing](../08_solo_founder_and_freelance/freelancing_and_consulting.md)); keep it limited so the search continues.

## 7. Getting back into the search

**A two-week reset.**

| Days | Do |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Checklist above; rest; tell close friends and family |
| 4–5 | Update resume and LinkedIn; write a short post or message to your network (template below) |
| 6–7 | Rebuild the target list ([search strategy](search_strategy.md#1-build-a-target-list)); list ten former colleagues to contact |
| 8–10 | Referral requests and outreach; restart DSA and design practice |
| 11–14 | First applications; weekly routine running ([pipeline](applications_pipeline.md#4-the-weekly-routine)) |

**Message to your network.**

```text
My role at Example SaaS was one of 120 affected by the reduction last week. Over 3 years I
built the feature store read path (p99 9 ms at 20k reads/s) and led the move of 40 batch jobs
to Airflow. I'm looking for backend or ML platform roles in Hyderabad or remote. If you know a
team that might be a fit, I'd be grateful for an introduction. Resume: <link>
```

**Explaining a layoff in interviews.** Keep it short, factual and forward-looking: "My team was part of a company-wide reduction in March. Before that I shipped X and Y. I'm looking for a team where I can do more of Z." Do not criticise the former employer.

## 8. Practice

1. Make a folder today with copies of your key employment and education documents, and a one-page list of your equity, insurance and PF details.
2. Calculate your monthly burn and how many months of runway you have.
3. Write your two-line layoff explanation and your network message now, so they are ready if you ever need them.
