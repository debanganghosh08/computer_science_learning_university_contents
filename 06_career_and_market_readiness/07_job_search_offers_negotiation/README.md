A job search is a pipeline with measurable stages: **targets → conversations → applications → interviews → offers → a decision**. Most people only measure the last step and feel lost. Track every stage weekly and you can see exactly where the search is failing and fix that stage.

## The numbers to watch each week

| Stage | Metric | If it is low, fix |
|---|---|---|
| Targets | New target companies added | Research ([search strategy](search_strategy.md#1-build-a-target-list)) |
| Outreach | Messages sent; reply rate | Message quality and targeting |
| Applications | Applications sent; response rate (any human reply or test) | Resume fit, channel (fewer cold portal applications, more referrals) |
| Interviews | First-round to next-round conversion | Interview skill: DSA, design, communication |
| Offers | Final rounds to offers | Final-round preparation, references, team fit questions |
